# Dotfall

A tiny one-screen game for kids. By day you grow a cluster of dots. By night you tap the purple blobs before they reach the gold home.

Inspired by territory-taking and day/build night/defend games. Original name, art, and rules.

## Open the game

No install. No account. No internet after you have the files.

1. Download or clone this folder.
2. Open `index.html` in a browser.
   - **Computer:** double-click `index.html`, or drag it onto Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.
   - **Phone:** copy the whole folder onto the device and tap `index.html`, or AirDrop / send the folder to yourself and open it.

That is the whole game: `index.html`, `style.css`, and `game.js`. Keep those three files together.

## How to play

- **Day:** tap a glowing neighbor to take it. Claims are instant.
- **Night:** tap the bouncing blobs. Your teal dots also slow them down.
- **Win:** last 3 nights, or take 20 dots (most of the field).
- **Lose:** a blob reaches the gold home. Tap **Play again**.

One line on screen is the reminder: *Tap a neighbor to take it* / *Tap the blobs!*
